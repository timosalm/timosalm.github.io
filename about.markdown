About
I'm a Master Solutions Architect at VMware Tanzu by Broadcom with over a decade of experience in customer-facing roles focused on modern applications, DevSecOps, and AI.

I regularly speak at conferences, executive briefings, and community events, translating complex technologies like AI into business impact.

As an ambassador for the Agentic AI Foundation and a Microsoft MVP recognized for his contributions to the developer community, I combine deep technical expertise with a passion for enabling organizations to innovate with AI and other emerging technologies.

Before joining Pivotal, which was later acquired by VMware and subsequently by Broadcom, I worked as a software architect and full-stack developer for consulting firms in the automotive industry.
